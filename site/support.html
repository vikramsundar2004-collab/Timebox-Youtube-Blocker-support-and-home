Support

Need help with Timebox?

Use these steps when YouTube is not blocking, the break timer looks wrong, or you need to understand the daily limit.

Contact

For support, open an issue on GitHub and include your browser version, operating system, and what happened.

Open a support issue

Do not post payment card details, passwords, or private account information in a public issue.

YouTube is not blocked

  1. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions.
  2. Make sure Timebox YouTube Blocker is enabled.
  3. Click Details and confirm site access is allowed for YouTube.
  4. Refresh YouTube or close and reopen the YouTube tab.
  5. If a break is active, wait for the timer to finish or click End break now.

The button says no breaks left

You get three 20-minute YouTube breaks per local day. After the third break, the blocker stays active until the next day.

The timer keeps running after closing Chrome

That is expected. The extension saves the break end time locally, so closing the popup does not pause or extend the break.

How to remove or disable the extension

Go to chrome://extensions, find Timebox YouTube Blocker, then disable it or click remove.
